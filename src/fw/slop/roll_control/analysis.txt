Model and fitting
roll'' = b*m - d*roll' + g*roll + bias
motor lag: tau*m' + m = differential_throttle(t-delay)
differential_throttle = (left - right)/2, throttle units 0..1000.
Angles in degrees; gyro X approximates roll rate for this single-axis fixture.
b, d and g are constrained nonnegative; positive g represents unstable gravity.
Integrate over 0.2 s before regression to avoid differentiating gyro noise.
Grid-search delay and lag; fit first two-thirds and score last third of each run.
Runs have equal weight, so the long periodic baseline does not dominate.
Exclude first second, windows containing |roll| >= 30 degrees, and telemetry gaps
>0.2 s. No endstop/contact samples are used for fitting. Interpolation adds no data.

Latest fit:
b=6.908, d=0.070, g=5.695, bias=57.254, delay=0.10 s, lag=0.24 s.
Validation RMSE: 14.27 deg/s for rate change over a 0.2-second window.
Near-best fits trade delay 0.08..0.16 s against lag 0.12..0.28 s. These are effective
system parameters, not a separately identified motor-only delay or time constant.
The original narrow-frequency recording could not identify unstable gravity;
new transients improve that, but closed-loop identifiability remains limited.

Gain search
First nominal-model optimum 1.5 / 0.8 / 1.25 failed in hardware.
After simulating measured 40 ms sampling and both endstop startup directions,
the nominal optimum 1.5 / 0.3 / 1.05 was measured and still oscillated badly.
A final robustness search adds 40 ms delay, 20% motor lag, and input-gain factors
0.8/1.0/1.2. These are chosen engineering stress scenarios, NOT confidence bounds.
No searched gains kept every simulated scenario inside 30 degrees after entry.
The ranked entries in tuning-result.json are least-bad diagnostics, not advice.
This does not prove PID cannot stabilize the rig; it shows this fitted model and
search do not support a robust recommendation. Startup contact/spin-up behavior
is especially uncertain because the linear fit intentionally excludes endstops.

Recorder incident and recovery
An initial attempt failed memory allocation before actuation. A smaller-buffer
retry lost communication; user restarted ESP. No onboard trace exists for that
failed attempt and its motor state was not verified before restart.
Recorder was then revised to reset the old asyncio loop, avoid the temporary
allocation, and use a 2 s hardware WDT. It was verified at zero throttle before
powered use. Successful experiments stop outputs, save data, and hard-reset into
normal firmware (PID defaults off). Fresh IMU data and motion limits are checked
locally. A previous loop-state problem is suspected, not proven as the hang cause.
MicroPython loop-reset documentation:
https://docs.micropython.org/en/latest/library/asyncio.html#asyncio.new_event_loop
