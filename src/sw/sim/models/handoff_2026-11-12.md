## 3. Roll-step ladder (first data past 35°), after the hold of step 2.4 is clean

- **Wind:** calm only, under about 1 m/s.
- **Height:** hold at 3 m (hover_climb 3, hover_ceiling 5).
- **Test settings:** pattern_tilt 42, pattern_distance 4, hover_gps_calibrate 5, roll_target_rate 360. Plus thrust_direction 0.8 and hover_kd 1.5 from section 2.
- **Pattern file:** print it with `uv run -m sw.sim.tilt_ladder --amplitudes 20,30,35 --width 0.4 --roll-rate 360 --dry > ladder35.json` and play it with sw/excite.py. Fly 20,30,35 first; 40 only on a later flight, after 35 was clean.

Sim results, with today's settings and the bands, 32 runs:

| wind | amplitudes | lost | true roll at the 35°/40° step (median, max) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 m/s | 20/30/35/40 | 0 | 31/36° and 36/41° |
| 2 m/s | 20/30/35 | 1 | |
| 2 m/s | 20/30/35/40 | 3 | |

- **Per step:** about 0.05 m of height lost, 1.1–2.4 m/s sideways, up to about 2.5 m to the side of the hold point.
- **Attitude estimate:** off by about 1.4° at the peak (up to about 5°).
- **Tip cut:** today's firmware stops the motors at 45°. The ladder is built to stay under it.

## 4. Not for today

- **Flip:** no flip in today's firmware. The flip controller exists only in the sim (flip.py), on the 11-09 airframe: the raised pivots and forward legs are not modelled. 1024 randomized sim flips from 3 m per case:
  - The 11-09 parameters stay 100% clean at the likely band offset (-2.5°), but 84% at -5°.
  - Retuned with the bands, models/flip_params_2026-11-12_bands.json (dbe3d6c) is 100% clean from 0 to -5°, with median height lost 0.00 m.
  - Porting the flip into the firmware is the remaining step. It also needs william/past-45 (tip_limit).
- **Firmware past 45°:** on branch william/past-45 (3 commits on 5cb48a0; fetch it the same way as william/cook). Not merged, not built, not reviewed for flight. Every new setting defaults to today's behavior.
  - 953862c: `tip_limit` setting (degrees, default 45, range 20-80) replaces TIP_LIMIT.
  - cc9f6cc: `bank_tilt` setting (degrees, default 0 = off). Past it, the attitude filter runs on the gyro alone. No measurable effect on 0.4 s steps.
  - 390f130: past `bank_tilt`, the collective is divided by the cosine of the tilt (at most 2x). In the sim, height lost at 40-55° goes from 0.03 m to 0.00 m.
  - The settings blob is now 1934 of 2048 bytes. Neither setting is on the dashboard.
  - It conflicts with 6ffdb22 in tilt_ladder.py (both add --settings); keep william/cook's version when merging.
- **Landings:** five of seven tipped backward after the touchdown cut on 2026-11-11. The cause isn't found; 22:59 had no bands.
