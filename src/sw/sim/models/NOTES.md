## The new ESCs

The thrust stand (sw/recordings/stand/2026-11-14T18-07-54; flip_esc.STAND and STAND_VOLTS) against the firmware's table times the 1.3 thrust scale: the stand gives 0.41-0.78 of it below hover throttle and 1.04-1.17 from 840 up. ## Quaternion attitude

The estimate is one quaternion (flight_math.c flight_attitude: the gyro turns it, the accelerometer pulls its up toward its own about a horizontal axis, so the heading stays the gyro's); the controller's roll and pitch errors are the tilt-prioritized error (Brescianini and D'Andrea) onto the setpoint at the drone's own heading, near level the Euler errors. The burst and the recovery fly the same estimate and the same error law in a rate loop (attitude_rates). Its cost on the host was 1.08 µs a tick against the Euler path's 0.52; on the ESP32 an estimated 10-25 µs of the 10 ms tick, to be measured on the flight controller.

## INDI

settings.def indi takes a share of the angular acceleration the gyro measures beyond the actuators' models (indi.c). In roll and yaw 0.5 steadies the hover (pitch rate RMS 7.8 -> 3.4 deg/s in the sim, roll 1.2 -> 0.7 degrees on the drone); much over 0.5 rings the assemblies' links at about 11 Hz. In pitch (indi_pitch) it doubled the drone's 2.6-3 Hz hover shake on 2026-11-15, and in the consolidated firmware, which takes indi_pitch for the hold, the burst and the recovery alike, 0.5 drops the bursts to 30-70% clean and the recovery to about 25% (below). Keep indi_pitch 0.

## Yaw in the flip

The bursts' yaw peaks are the rotors' spin momentum changing hands as the motors' split reverses (-dH_z/dt, H_z = rotor_momentum (√T_right - √T_left)); the differential tilt alone cannot take it back. yaw_ff 1.6 (rotor_momentum 0.018 over the yaw inertia; flight fits put rotor_momentum at 0.006-0.03, the prop's geometry at about 0.014) feeds it forward, judged on the worst rotor_momentum band below 0.03.

**The physics, all in physical form** (new optional terms in airframe.py hub_aero and rigid_step.c, parity 1e-13; every older model flies as before):
- The rotors' in-plane force at their hubs, rotor_drag_h √(T T₀) times the air's in-plane speed at the hub (the body's velocity, its rates about the CoM and the assembly's own swing), acting flap_lever up the shaft (the rigid prop's flapping hub moment): 0.0267 s/m, 0.142 m. One pair of numbers is the pitch and yaw damping, the speed moments at any attitude and speed and the translational rotor drag; it replaces edgewise_damping, vx_drag/vy_drag and their heights, speed_moment2, speed_range, pitch_pendulum, pitch_wake and rigid.py's SPEED_RANGE clip and ATTITUDE_RANGE fade (no attitude term is left, so nothing is clipped). Its consequence is the hovering rotorcraft's unstable pitch-velocity mode: a tilt's forward force builds speed, and the speed's moment about the CoM (12 rad/s² per m/s) cancels the tilt's torque below about 1 Hz.
- The axial inflow at each hub, rotor_inflow √(T T₀) times the axial speed (0.37 s/m: the roll damping), of which a steady climb keeps inflow_steady 0.21 once the unloaded motor has sped up over inflow_recovery 0.19 s. The 07:03 log shows why both: the specific force stayed 1.75-1.84 g while the climb went 0.7 → 2.3 m/s, which a single inflow coefficient big enough for the roll damping cannot do (the sim climbed 1.0 m where the drone climbed 2.2 m).
- The tilt's pitch authority 1.57 times the CAD geometry's (tilt_authority). Physically the CoM about 25 mm lower than the CAD estimate (0.018 m above the tube, from volumes, not weighed) or the pitch inertia about 0.0033 kg·m² instead of 0.00525: the two act alike in pitch and the flights do not separate them (a CoM-height fit ran to its bound with the H-force's lever riding along). A hang test (CoM height) or a bifilar swing (pitch inertia) would.
- Servos: dead time 75 ms left, 55 ms right (from the logged command; the bench's first motion, unloaded, 40-50 and 30 ms), an underdamped loop wn 33 rad/s, zeta 0.22, play 0.30 / 0.15 degrees, the link 8.9 N·m/rad with 0.09 N·m·s/rad, and the assemblies' swing reaction at the CAD's inertia (servo_inertia 0: a servo motor's reflected inertia loads its own loop but reacts on the body only as J_m N, not J_m N²; fits that freed it took 10 times the CAD's, which rang the hold at 1.5 Hz in the closed loop).
- A disturbance state (the plant's "observer"): an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck torque per axis, correlation 26 s, prior sd 6.8 / 12 / 7.9 rad/s² roll / pitch / yaw, estimated by a Kalman filter from the log alone over each prediction's warm-up (airborne ticks only) and carried into the prediction. It is what the hovers' wandering roll torque is: wind, the battery's seat, the ESCs; not deterministic physics.
- Kept from before, refitted: wash 0.75 (yaw's tilt authority 0.25 of the geometric), prop_torque 0.012, roll_from_yaw 0.056, yaw_from_roll -0.017, the new ESCs' motors 54 ms late with 90 ms lag (the old 92 ms). rotor_momentum 0.0028 with momentum_tilt (prop_momentum's physics folded in).

**Rejected candidates** (each with the evidence):
- Loop latency from late ticks: the logs' ticks are on time (above).
- The 2x authority as an identification artifact only: the frequency response measures it where noise cannot bias it (coherence 0.99).
- Play as the source of the shake's phase: the shake's frequency and the response are the same with the bands taking up the play; play only sets the amplitude. Fits that leaned on play (0.9-1.6 degrees) shook twice the drone's amplitude without bands and 0.4 Hz fast with them.
- A large swing reaction (servo_inertia 0.001-0.003, 4-10 times the CAD assembly's): fits the response's gain at 2-3.5 Hz but rang the closed loop at 1.5 Hz, and is not physical.
- A lower CoM alone (com_z free): ran to its bound (77-80 mm below the CAD's).
- A soft linkage (link_k 0.3) for the response's rising gain: misfit 0.75 against 0.47.
- Yaw authority (wash) as burst30's failure with the props' full momentum: burst30 still fails at wash 0.38 or 0.6.

## Servo dead-time predictor (2026-11-15, william/predictor)

**What it is.** The pitch loop carries 80-95 ms of delay at its 2.3-2.5 Hz shake, and 55-75 ms of it is the servos' dead time. A Smith predictor (fw/drone/main/indi.c indi_predict) runs the servo commands already on their way (INDI_SERVO_DELAY - 1 ticks of them) through INDI's actuator estimate and adds the pitch and pitch rate they will make to what the hold's P and damping terms, the burst's and the recovery's attitude laws act on; the integrator keeps the error now. Its model is INDI's (60 ms dead time, the horn at wn 37.5 and zeta 0.87, the motors' lag, the assemblies' swing reaction), scaled by PREDICT_AUTHORITY 0.7, plus what the drone's pitch acceleration has beyond that model (the speed moment that cancels the tilt below 1 Hz, the CoM), low-passed over 50 ms and held over the horizon. Setting pitch_predict: the share taken (0 off), faded in and out with INDI (the hold at least 0.3 m up), so nothing changes on the ground. Cost: about 1.5 times INDI's update on the host (about 160 us more per tick on the ESP32 by the timing agent's INDI figure, not measured on the board); 0.5 KB of IRAM (6.1 KB left).

**Checked against the drone, not the sim.** sw/sim/predict_check.py runs each recording's commands through the firmware's estimate and sets the predicted change in pitch rate over the horizon against the gyro's own change. On 2026-11-15's hovers (2026-11-12 01:07 and 2026-11-15 04:12 held out): 1.5-4 Hz slope 1.0-1.2, correlation 0.92-0.98; below 1.5 Hz 0.84-1.12 and 0.85-0.98 (without the term beyond the model, below 1.5 Hz the slope was 0.25-0.5: the speed moment). Above 4 Hz it has no skill. The horizon is right: one tick later only lowers the correlation; 5 and 7 tick dead times fit worse than 6. ASSEMBLY_RATIO 0.18 (was 0.27): 0.27 fits 1.5-4 Hz best (correlation 0.95-0.99) but foretells 1.5-2 times the drone's motion above 4 Hz, and in the sim a predictor that overstates the fast reaction feeds it back as an 8 Hz oscillation; at 0.18 the prediction is the drone's size above 4 Hz.
