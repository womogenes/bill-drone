## How to score it

The flight is held out: compare the log's roll peak and time, hand-back, the nose's largest excursion and its time, the pitch rate peak, the height lost and gained (sw/sim/burst_closed.py prepare and summary give the log's measures), and whether it was clean. The plant passes if the roll peak and hand-back fall inside the bands above, the nose excursion is within 14-37 deg and the height lost within 0.1-0.5 m. If the nose goes past 40 degrees or the drone loses over 0.6 m, the plant is wrong about the post-burst pitch (its gyroscopic coupling and pitch loop margin first: models/NOTES.md "One plant", open questions).
