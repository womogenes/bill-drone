Symmetric four-bolt camera bottom clamp — 2026-10-19

One printed continuous slotted bar on a 48 x 24 x 3 mm base; total height 11.3 mm.
Uses all four existing M3 deck holes, 38 x 12 mm pattern (X +/-19; Y -36/-24).
Mount is mirrored front-to-back about Y=-30; both jaws have identical nut pockets and screw bores.
Install only TWO M2 x 8 mm flat-ended set screws and TWO standard M2 nuts in the non-antenna/camera-facing jaw. Leave the opposite jaw's screw pockets empty.
Four M3 x 10 screws and matching nuts attach it to the unchanged deck; check actual nut engagement.
